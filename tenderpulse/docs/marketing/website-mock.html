Quick answers

Where do TenderBell tenders come from?

TenderBell monitors Zimbabwe’s official PRAZ e-GP publisher and links every alert back to the original notice.

How often will I receive alerts?

Free subscribers receive a Monday email digest. Paid subscribers receive alerts after each TenderBell monitoring run.

Can I receive alerts through WhatsApp?

Yes. Pro and Max subscribers can optionally enable WhatsApp delivery alongside email.

What is a tender category match?

It is a saved business category TenderBell uses to identify relevant tenders, reminders and award notices.

What is included in the Max plan?

Max includes everything in Pro, unlimited category matches, the TenderBell Market Insights Portal and market opportunity insights.
