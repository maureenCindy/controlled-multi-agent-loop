Privacy note

A plain-English summary of what we collect and how we use it during the pre-launch waitlist and early MVP stage. This is not a substitute for a full, counsel-reviewed privacy policy — see the note at the bottom of this page.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist or register for alerts, we collect your email address and, if you choose to share them, your sector, province, and company name. We don't ask for or collect anything beyond what the sign-up form requests.

What we use it for

We use your email only to notify you about Zimbabwe PRAZ e-GP tenders that match your stated interests, and to send occasional build/launch updates you've signed up for. We don't use your details for anything else, and signup is required before we ever contact you — nobody is emailed who hasn't submitted the form themselves.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or share your email, sector, province, or company with third parties. Tender alerts we send link back to the official PRAZ e-GP listing for the tender itself — we don't host or resell bid documents.

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your details at any time by reaching out on X (@tenderpulse_zw). We'll delete your waitlist/subscriber record on request.

MVP-stage disclaimer

TenderPulse is a pre-launch MVP with no real subscriber data flowing through paid features yet. This page is a basic, honest consent/fair-use note written for that stage — it is not vetted legal advice and has not been reviewed by counsel. Before handling real user data at production scale, this note must be replaced with a proper privacy policy reviewed by a lawyer familiar with Zimbabwean and any other applicable data-protection law.
